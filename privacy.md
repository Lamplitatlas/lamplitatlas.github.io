# Privacy Policy – The Nightwatch Atlas upload tool

The channel's private upload tool is used only by the owner of the YouTube channel The Nightwatch Atlas (@TheNightwatchAtlas) to upload and manage videos on that channel.

**Data accessed:** the tool uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos, thumbnails, captions and metadata, and to read basic channel information for the owner's own channel only.

**Storage and sharing:** no data from other users is collected. Access tokens are stored securely and used only to manage the owner's channel. No data is sold or shared with third parties.

**Revoking access:** at any time via https://myaccount.google.com/connections
